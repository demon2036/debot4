# Narrative and entry framework v0.1

This document separates three questions that are often incorrectly collapsed into one:

1. Is the story real and bound to this exact contract?
2. Is the story capable of spreading now?
3. Is the current market position still tradable after latency and costs?

## 1. Narrative quality

A high-quality candidate needs auditable facts rather than a model-generated score alone:

- **Source quality:** original celebrity/company/project post outranks screenshots, reposts, and aggregator alerts.
- **Token binding:** full CA in the original chain of evidence, an official account linking the CA, or a separately verified deployer/account relationship.
- **Freshness:** an external event or new propagation wave inside six hours. Older memes need a new secondary catalyst.
- **Simplicity:** the idea can be repeated in one sentence and mapped to a recognizable person, object, event, or community identity.
- **Novelty:** new event or new framing, not only recycling a successful ticker.
- **Independent confirmation:** unrelated X/TG sources, a fresh DeBot buy, and on-chain activity count separately only when they are genuinely independent.
- **Propagation:** increasing unique authors and communities over time is stronger than many copies from one cluster.

The LLM returns structured claims, entities, narrative hypotheses, counter-evidence, and uncertainty. It never supplies missing URLs or decides the order action.

## 2. Hard rejection conditions

Any one of the following prevents a paper buy:

- exact BSC contract is unresolved or same-name contracts remain ambiguous;
- no verifiable public source URL for the claimed narrative;
- evidence became available after the decision timestamp;
- honeypot/sell test failure, owner controls or taxes outside the configured bounds;
- stale/unavailable market quote, inadequate quote reserve, or unacceptable price impact;
- current price has already moved beyond the configured chase limit since the evidence became actionable;
- the only positive input is a DeBot/KOL label or a single vendor smart-wallet tag;
- the strategy would need a future candle high/low to obtain the claimed fill.

## 3. Entry window

The first version seeks a confirmation window, not the absolute bottom:

1. Fresh public evidence appears and is bound to the exact CA.
2. A second independent signal arrives before obvious acceleration: a distinct author/community, a timestamped DeBot buy, or fresh on-chain buyer expansion.
3. A live sellability/liquidity check passes.
4. Price is above a small momentum floor but below the chase ceiling.
5. The first executable quote after bot/human latency determines the paper fill.

The six-hour narrative window is supported by the current positive-catalyst sample. Price-change, liquidity, tax, and impact cutoffs are safety hypotheses until real shadow and paper observations calibrate them.

## 4. Smart wallets

External labels start with zero positive trading weight. A wallet can become followable only after a frozen out-of-sample history demonstrates positive realized expectancy after a realistic 10–30 second following delay, gas, tax, slippage, failed exits, and cluster de-duplication. Creator-linked, bundle, sniper, wash, or follower-dump behavior is a risk feature, not alpha.

## 5. What the current history cannot prove

The existing set is selected around movers and therefore cannot establish win rate or causal effect. It lacks a complete non-mover control set, actual signal-arrival timestamps, executable decision-time depth, failed-sell observations, and full transaction costs. Extreme reserve-ratio percentage moves are treated as thin-pool anomalies rather than realizable returns.
